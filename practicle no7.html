Student Help Portal

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I register for a course? +
Students can register for a course through the college student portal using their login credentials.
2. Where can I check my examination timetable? +
The examination timetable is available on the college notice board and student portal.
3. How can I download study material? +
Study material can be downloaded from the respective subject section of the student portal.
4. What should I do if I forget my password? +
Click on the forgot password option on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.
5. How can I contact the college department? +
Students can contact the department office during working hours or send an email to the department.
